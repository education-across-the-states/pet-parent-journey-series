# The Pet Parent Journey Series

Twelve books covering the full companion-animal ownership lifecycle, published by PawSmart Education. Each finished book is published here as it is completed.

1. **The First Decision** — Choosing & Preparing for Your New Pet (`books/book-01-the-first-decision/`)
2. **The First 30 Days** — Surviving & Bonding Through the Adjustment Period (`books/book-02-the-first-30-days/`)
3. **Speaking Their Language** — Understanding Behavior & Communication (`books/book-03-speaking-their-language/`)
4. **Foundations of Training** — Building Good Habits That Last a Lifetime (`books/book-04-foundations-of-training/`)
5. **Fueling Their Life** — Nutrition & Physical Health Fundamentals (`books/book-05-fueling-their-life/`)
6. **The Growing Years** — Navigating Adolescence & Behavioral Challenges (`books/book-06-the-growing-years/`)
7. **Life Together** — Travel, Adventure & Everyday Life With Your Pet (`books/book-07-life-together/`)
8. **The Steady Years** — Maintaining Health & Happiness in Adulthood (`books/book-08-the-steady-years/`)
9. **The Graying Years** — Caring for Your Senior Pet (`books/book-09-the-graying-years/`)
10. **Difficult Decisions** — Navigating Serious Illness & End-of-Life Choices (`books/book-10-difficult-decisions/`)
11. **The Final Goodbye** — The Days Surrounding Loss (`books/book-11-the-final-goodbye/`)
12. **When They Leave Pawprints on Your Heart** — A Complete Guide to Grief, Healing & Remembrance (`books/book-12-when-they-leave-pawprints-on-your-heart/`)
